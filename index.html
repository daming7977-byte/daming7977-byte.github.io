About

My research asks how cells dynamically coordinate protein synthesis with membrane insertion, translocation, and quality control.

At Kyoto University, I combine bacterial genetics and biochemistry with Deep Mutational Scanning, next-generation sequencing, ribosome profiling, and computational modeling. This multiscale approach helps uncover regulatory principles—from arrest-peptide behavior to proteome-wide translational pausing.

02

Research

Selected directions spanning experiment, high-throughput biology, and computation.

01 / Genome scale

Translational pausing during ER stress

Ribosome-profiling analysis of 5,028 transmembrane domains to investigate selective pausing remodeling and the contribution of eS7 ubiquitination.

02 / Computation

Protein representations & prediction

Comparative modeling of 997 yeast membrane proteins using engineered features, ESM2 embeddings, AlphaFold-derived descriptors, and interpretable machine learning.

03 / Mechanism

Regulation & quality control

Genetics, biochemistry, DMS/NGS, and protein engineering applied to arrest peptides, the YidC/Sec machinery, and the intramembrane protease RseP.
